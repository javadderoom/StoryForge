## 🎯 1. The Quality & Co-Creation Mandate

Quality in AfsanehSaz is not measured by raw text quantity or speed, but by **literary depth, atmospheric gravitas, and author collaboration**:

### A. The 4 Pillars of Literary Content Quality
1. **Few-Shot Literary Exemplars**: Prompts assemble gold-standard excerpts written in high-register literary Persian (Farsi) and evocative English rather than generic robotic instructions.
2. **Strict Anti-Cliché & Trope Banning**: Prohibits overused generic AI tropes (*"tapestry of fate"*, *"whispers of the forgotten"*, *"a cloaked shadowy figure"*, *"ancient evil awakens"*), demanding concrete sensory details, moral ambiguity, and philosophical friction.
3. **Multi-Angle Variant Generation (3 Stylistic Angles)**: When generating ideas, the AI synthesizes 3 distinct creative interpretations:
   - *Option A: The Grim & Tragic (High emotional/physical cost)*
   - *Option B: The Mythic & Ancient (Cosmological weight and archaic mystery)*
   - *Option C: The Machiavellian & Political (Intrigue, leverage, and betrayal)*
4. **Deterministic Context Grounding**: The AI anchors all facts to existing World Laws, Factions, Eras, and Locations stored in the World Bible.

---

### B. The "Interactive Workshop" (Back-and-Forth Co-Creation Loop)
Great world-building is an iterative dialogue, not a single one-click stamp:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                             The Interactive Workshop Loop                                │
├──────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Initial Draft/Variants│ 2. Conversational Dialogue│ 3. Surgical Merge & Commit        │
├──────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────┤
│ AI generates 3 distinct  │ Author asks for tweaks:   │ Author picks best parts, reviews  │
│ stylistic angles         │ "Make her colder, add a   │ visual diff, and commits cleanly  │
│ (Grim / Mythic / Intrigue│ blood debt to Lord Vesper"│ into the World Bible.             │
└──────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **Inline "💬 Workshop with AI" Drawer on Every Entity Card**: Located on every NPC, Location, Relic, Law, or Deities card across the studio.
* **Conversational Refinement**: Chat back and forth with the AI directly about that single entity without losing context.
* **Live Variant Comparison**: Compare versions side-by-side, swap out individual fields (e.g. keep Name from A, Secret from B), and commit only when 100% satisfied.
* **Draft Revision Stack**: Undo/redo through the conversational iteration history.

## 🔬 4. Quality Assurance & Evaluation Framework

### A. The "Lore Anchor Score" (LAS)
Every generation must pass an automated scoring metric ensuring at least **2 direct references** to existing World Bible entities (e.g. citing an established faction, tying a relic to a historical era, or placing an NPC in an existing location).

### B. Dual-Language Literary Verification
* **Persian (Farsi)**: Prompts enforce classical literary register, evocative metaphors, correct cultural cadence, and zero transliterated English slang. All UI containers enforce strict BiDi and LTR-wrapping for symbols and numbers.
* **English**: Prompts enforce atmospheric prose with sensory depth, vivid verbs, and grim fantasy diction.

### C. Zod Schema Integrity & Fallbacks
If an AI response fails schema validation, the parser executes an automated one-shot schema repair prompt before returning a graceful fallback.
